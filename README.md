# Chum Chimes

**Never miss a friend in passing!** Chum Chimes plays a friendly little chime when one of your guildies or friends is in the same zone as you, and a different one when they're right near you. Say hi, group up, wave as you pass, or strike up some impromptu RP!

Works in **Retail** and **WoW Forever**.

## Features

- **"In my zone" chime.** When a guildie or friend is in the same zone as you, you get a chime and a line in chat, like "Tom just showed up in Silvermoon City!" It works both ways: they arrive, or you do.
- **"Right near me" chime.** A different sound when one of them is close by, or when you mouse over or target them. "Psst! Tom is right by you!" You choose how close counts: Close (about 28 yards), Very close (about 10 yards), or as soon as their nameplate shows.
- **One summary when you arrive somewhere busy.** Zone into a city full of guildies and you get one line and one chime, not a dozen: "Your chums in Stormwind: Tom, Johnny, Nathan and 3 more!"
- **No spam.** Each person chimes once, then stays quiet for 10 minutes. Change that to anything from 1 to 60 minutes.
- **Guild, WoW friends and Battle.net friends.** Each one has its own on/off switch.
- **Clickable names.** Click a name in chat to whisper them right away.
- **Knows when to hush.** Stays quiet in dungeons, raids, delves and battlegrounds, and skips people already in your group.
- **Pick your sounds.** Choose a different sound for each kind of chime, or none at all.
- **Light as a feather.** It only wakes up when the game says something changed. Two small exceptions: a "where is everyone?" request every 2 minutes for guild and friends zones (the game doesn't announce those, and you can turn it off), and when a chum's nameplate shows up far away, a once-a-second check on just that one person until they come close or wander off.

## Friendly nameplates

The "right near me" chime notices a chum walking up when their nameplate appears, so turn on friendly nameplates (**Shift-V**, or the button in the settings). Mouseover and target chimes work either way.

## Commands

- `/chum`: open settings
- `/chum test`: hear and see a test "in my zone" chime
- `/chum test near`: hear and see a test "right near me" chime
- `/chum test summary`: see a test summary line
- `/chum who`: list the chums in your zone right now
- `/chum plates`: turn on friendly nameplates
- `/chum on` / `/chum off`: turn all chimes on or off
- Minimap button and addon compartment (the puzzle piece by the minimap): left-click for settings, right-click to hear the test chimes

## Settings

Find them under **Options → AddOns → NerdyBertie → Chum Chimes**: who to chime for, when to chime, how close counts as "right near me," the sound for each kind of chime, the on-screen line, the quiet time between chimes, the minimap button, and test buttons for everything.

The AddOns and Options menus group my addons together, so you can find them all under the NerdyBertie heading.
