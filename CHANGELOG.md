# Chum Chimes

## v1.0.0

### New
- A friendly chime when a guildie or friend is in the same zone as you, whether they arrive or you do.
- A different chime when one of them is right near you, or when you mouse over or target them. Pick how close counts: Close (about 28 yards), Very close (about 10 yards), or as soon as their nameplate shows.
- Arrive somewhere busy and you get one summary line instead of a chime for each person: "Your chums in Dornogal: Tom, Johnny and 3 more!"
- No spam: each person chimes once, then stays quiet for 10 minutes (you can change that).
- Chimes for guild members, WoW friends and Battle.net friends, each with its own on/off switch.
- Names in chat are clickable, so you can whisper right away.
- Stays quiet in dungeons, raids, delves and battlegrounds, and skips people already in your group.
- Pick the sound for each kind of chime.
- Minimap button: left-click for settings, right-click to hear the test chimes.
- Settings under Options → AddOns → NerdyBertie → Chum Chimes.
